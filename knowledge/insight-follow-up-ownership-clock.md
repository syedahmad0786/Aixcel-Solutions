# The Ownership Clock for an AI Follow Up Queue

A message can be ready while the next action remains unowned. The ownership clock measures the time from a qualified signal to accepted human ownership, not only the time needed to create a draft.

The clock starts when a signal meets the team rule. It stops only when a named person accepts a specific action and due time. Waiting, offered, accepted, and exception remain separate states, and every exception has its own named owner.

AI may collect context, group duplicate signals, propose priority, draft the next action, and surface missing fields. A person verifies the account, permission, commitment, due time, and accepted owner.

The ownership clock is Ahmad Bukhari's operating synthesis informed by current HubSpot, Salesforce, Microsoft, and NIST sources. It is not a vendor feature, official standard, universal service level, or performance claim.
