# AEO Services and Free AI Visibility Audit

AiXCEL uses AIEO for the wider AI-engine visibility system, AEO as the simple public name for the managed service, and GEO for the generative-answer lens. They are connected parts of the same offer, and all of them build on sound technical search foundations rather than replacing SEO.

The public starting offer is a free AEO audit. A visitor enters a website first, then provides the minimum contact and commercial context needed for a human-reviewed report. The audit can include a scoped 0-100 visibility score, competitor and share-of-voice comparison, observed mentions and citations, answer position, sentiment and brand-fact checks, technical site issues, and prioritized content actions. It records the prompt set, platform, date, market context, sources, and limitations. It is not a universal rank or permanent visibility score.

The managed service can cover prompt and platform monitoring, competitor analysis, citation and source tracking, technical site audits, entity and schema fixes, answer-ready content planning and production, white-labelled reporting, and lead measurement. Platform coverage can include ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, and other supported answer surfaces. Exact coverage is confirmed for each scope.

The delivery platform is white-labelled, but the public promise, recommendations, client communication, evidence boundary, and service accountability remain AiXCEL's. Public copy must not expose vendor branding, copy vendor testimonials, or imply a placement guarantee.

AiXCEL does not guarantee that ChatGPT, Google, Perplexity, or another external answer engine will rank, cite, recommend, or send traffic to a business. It does not claim special AI-only schema, manufacture reviews or citations, or convert an academic benchmark into a client performance promise. Success is measured through separate evidence states: observed representation, citations, identifiable referrals, consented leads, qualified bookings, opportunities, and wins.
