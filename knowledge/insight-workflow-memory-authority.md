# A Personal AI Should Remember the Method, Not Yesterday's Authority

Workflow memory can help a personal AI reuse a proven preparation method. It does not establish that an old fact, permission, instruction, or commitment remains current.

The workflow stencil preserves the sequence, structure, approved source categories, failure questions, and stop point. Four current inputs must be checked again before consequential action: source, access, instruction, and commitment.

Memory may prepare the work. Current evidence and a named person authorize what may leave the workspace.

The stencil model is Ahmad Bukhari's operating synthesis informed by current workflow memory, provenance, and agent architecture research. It is not a vendor feature, official standard, safety guarantee, compliance guarantee, or measured business outcome.
