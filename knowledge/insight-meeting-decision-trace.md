# Preserve the Decision Trace in AI Meeting Notes

The meeting summary can name the right topic and still create the wrong next step. A useful acceptance test asks whether proposals, objections, conditions, revisions, owners, dates, and confirmed commitments survived accurately.

The article uses four states: proposed, challenged, revised, and confirmed. Only a confirmed item can become a candidate action. A person still verifies the qualifier, owner, date, recipients, source moment, and downstream action before anything reaches a CRM or a client.

A representative pilot includes deliberate traps for negation, a changed date, a condition, and an owner switch. The scorecard tracks state accuracy, qualifier retention, owner and date accuracy, unauthorized action attempts, and review time.

The four state trace is Ahmad Bukhari's operating synthesis informed by current Google, Microsoft, Zoom, and NIST sources. It is not a vendor feature, an official NIST standard, or legal advice.
