# Similarity Needs a Receipt Before It Changes Work

Embeddings can find records that are close to a question in meaning. Semantic closeness does not establish that a record is current, permitted, complete, or correct for a business exception.

A retrieval receipt records the exact question, selected record, version, access scope, retrieved passage, similarity rank, filters, rejected candidates, exception state, and decision owner before an AI answer changes consequential work.

AI can find and organize candidate evidence. Explicit controls can narrow the source set. A person remains accountable for policy meaning, exceptions, commitments, and final action.

The retrieval receipt is Ahmad Bukhari's operating synthesis informed by current Google, OpenAI, pgvector, and NIST sources. It is not a vendor feature, official standard, accuracy guarantee, or compliance guarantee.
