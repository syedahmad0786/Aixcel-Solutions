Discovery and verification are different steps.

A search snippet, abstract, full article, and original dataset support different levels of confidence. A useful brief tells the reader which level was available instead of treating every citation as equivalent.

What to put into the workflow.

  • Label discovery-only references.
  • Read the primary material behind important claims where available.
  • Record access limits and missing details.
  • Scale the conclusion to the material actually inspected.

A concrete example.

An abstract may describe the study objective and reported result. It usually does not provide enough detail to reproduce the method or audit the data.

What to take away.

Name the decision, keep the relevant evidence, and make the human responsibility clear. Start with a small, inspectable workflow before expanding what the system is allowed to do.
