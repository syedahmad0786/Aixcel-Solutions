Clear source material matters more than the acronym.

AEO, GEO, and similar labels are used for work intended to improve how information appears in AI-assisted discovery. The practical starting point is a site that accurately explains the business, its services, and its evidence.

What to put into the workflow.

  • Make the service and audience easy to understand.
  • Keep structured data consistent with visible content.
  • Use original, verifiable examples and clear source attribution.
  • Measure specific observations without promising citations or rankings.

A concrete example.

A page explaining a real CRM integration with its scope and limitations gives a buyer more useful information than a broad claim to be the best AI agency.

What to take away.

Name the decision, keep the relevant evidence, and make the human responsibility clear. Start with a small, inspectable workflow before expanding what the system is allowed to do.

More on search.

Read Google’s structured-data guidelines and site-migration guidance for the technical website work. AI-answer observations remain separate from traffic and sales outcomes.
