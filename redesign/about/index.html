About AiXCEL

A small studio, close to the work.

AiXCEL is a founder-led automation and AI systems studio led by Ahmad Bukhari.

Led by a builder.

AiXCEL is led by Ahmad Bukhari, whose background spans sales, CRM operations, team training, and systems delivery. The work stays close to the business process and the people responsible for it.

The studio works across no-code integration platforms and custom agentic systems. Projects are scoped around a useful operational outcome, with collaborators brought in only where the work calls for them.

How we approach the work.

  • Start with a specific business process and a person who owns it.
  • Use existing tools when they can do the job well.
  • Make permissions, review, and failure handling part of the design.
  • Test realistic cases, document what was verified, and make handover usable.

Who the work is for.

Service businesses, agencies, and internal teams that have a real workflow to improve, access to the relevant systems, and someone ready to own the result.
