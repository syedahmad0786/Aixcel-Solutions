The job.

Compare seller confidence with buyer evidence and dated commitments before a forecast decision.

What to inspect.

Compare forecast confidence with the actual buyer language, stakeholder coverage, and next dated commitment.

How to evaluate it.

  • Read the scenario and source data before interpreting the result.
  • Inspect how the system handles missing or conflicting evidence.
  • Look for the human decision point and the permitted actions.
  • Check the deployment’s current mode and limitations.
