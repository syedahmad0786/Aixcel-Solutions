# Systems Desk walkthrough scripts

These three scripts explain the implemented Systems Desk beta without extending its claims. Each is written for a 60–90 second presenter-led walkthrough with screen capture from `/systems-desk`.

## Recording rules

- Read only the text labelled **Spoken copy**. Shot and on-screen cues are silent production instructions; do not ad-lib product claims.
- Capture the real interface. Do not simulate a successful answer, account state, or deployment condition that is not working at recording time.
- Use fictional business and workflow details in every form and chat demonstration. Never record passwords, tokens, private client information, health information, or payment data.
- Keep the source rail, agent selector, human gates, consent language, and privacy page legible when they are referenced.
- Preserve the stated limits: approved corpus only, no live web search, no autonomous actions, no pricing or outcome promises, and no saved chat transcript in the initial release.

## Scripts

1. `01-evidence-grounded-answers.md` — question-to-source answer path.
2. `02-three-agent-jobs.md` — Systems Auditor, Ask AiXCEL, and Solution Mapper.
3. `03-data-saved-and-not-saved.md` — account, problem-brief, usage, and chat-data handling.

Before publishing, record against the deployed build and verify that the visible behavior still matches the spoken copy.
