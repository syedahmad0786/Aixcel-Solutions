# Walkthrough 02 — The three agent jobs

**Target duration:** 75–85 seconds
**Format:** Presenter voice with agent-selector and catalog screen capture
**Recording data:** Use a fictional operating problem only

## 00:00–00:10 — Introduce the selector

**Shot cue:** Start on the three mode buttons. Move the pointer across each without submitting a question.

**On screen:** `THREE JOBS · ONE EVIDENCE STANDARD`

**Spoken copy:** “Systems Desk has three agents, but they are not three personalities. Each is a bounded job with its own instruction, evidence context, and human gate.”

## 00:10–00:26 — Systems Auditor

**Shot cue:** Select Systems Auditor. Highlight its catalog card and the prompt asking where work or revenue is stuck.

**On screen:** `SYSTEMS AUDITOR · DIAGNOSE`

**Spoken copy:** “Systems Auditor is for an unclear bottleneck or fragile handoff. It separates stated facts from hypotheses, identifies likely workflow, ownership, data, and control gaps, names missing evidence, and recommends the smallest useful next check.”

## 00:26–00:42 — Ask AiXCEL

**Shot cue:** Select Ask AiXCEL. Show the linked services, cases, insights, and policy sources in the knowledge section.

**On screen:** `ASK AIXCEL · VERIFY`

**Spoken copy:** “Ask AiXCEL explains the company’s published services, methods, cases, operating boundaries, and evidence. It is the right job for service-fit questions, and it should say plainly when the approved corpus does not verify a requested capability.”

## 00:42–00:59 — Solution Mapper

**Shot cue:** Select Solution Mapper. Overlay a simple route: stages, decisions, owners, exceptions, controls, evidence.

**On screen:** `SOLUTION MAPPER · MAP`

**Spoken copy:** “Solution Mapper starts after the problem is defined. It turns that context into proposed stages, decision rules, owners, exceptions, human approvals, observability, and acceptance evidence. It describes a bounded design; it never implies that implementation already happened.”

## 00:59–01:17 — Show the shared boundaries

**Shot cue:** Scroll across the three catalog cards and pause on each Human gate row.

**On screen:** `HUMANS OWN CONSEQUENCES`

**Spoken copy:** “All three use the same approved knowledge standard and the signed-in user’s saved problem brief. They do not browse, call tools, change another system, set a price, or commit AiXCEL to work. Scope, access, commercial recommendations, credentials, and write actions stay with people.”

## 01:17–01:25 — Close on the catalog title

**Shot cue:** Return to the full agent catalog and hold on the three cards.

**On screen:** `CHOOSE THE JOB, THEN INSPECT THE EVIDENCE.`

**Spoken copy:** “Choose the job that matches the decision you need, then inspect the evidence behind the answer.”
